How to verify

How to verify what
this tool tells you.

This tool can help you…

  • Structure an unstructured question into regime, destination, recipient, ETL and evidence questions.
  • Surface the Excluded Technologies List and the five Australian criteria at the point of decision.
  • Assemble an evidence pack and a list of questions for your export-control contact.
  • Understand why a given pathway was reached, via a cited decision trail.

This tool cannot — and must not — do

  • Make a formal export-control classification (USML / DSGL / CCL determination).
  • Determine whether a specific item is on the ETL in a binding sense.
  • Grant, waive or substitute for any licence, permit or authorisation.
  • Replace advice from Defence Export Controls, DDTC, BIS or a qualified lawyer.
  • Account for your specific contract terms, security clearances or end-use undertakings.

Verify in four steps

  1. Classify — confirm the item’s USML / DSGL / EAR status (or seek a commodity-jurisdiction / classification request).
  2. Check the ETL — review Supplement No. 2 to 22 CFR Part 126 and Australia’s Excluded Goods & Technologies List.
  3. Confirm eligibility — verify Authorised-User registration (DDTC DECCS / MADE portal) and the five Australian criteria.
  4. Escalate — where classification is unclear, ETL/sensitive areas are touched, or third-country transfer is involved, obtain written authorisation or a legal opinion before proceeding.
