The host region is the case study

Desalination supplies 42% of the UAE's drinking water and 90–99% in Bahrain, Kuwait and Qatar. In 2026, plants in Bahrain, Kuwait and on Iran's Qeshm Island were reported hit during the US–Israel war with Iran, with responsibility disputed.

Why it matters

Abu Dhabi will discuss water security while living it. Expect protection of water infrastructure in conflict to surface in the Water for Cooperation dialogue, which already names water as a target and tool of coercion.

Violence over water is accelerating

The Pacific Institute recorded 420 events in 2024, nearly 20% more than 2023 and 78% more than 2022, against 24 in 2000.

Shared water is under-governed

153 countries share 313 transboundary basins and 426 aquifer systems, yet only 43 have arrangements covering 90% or more of those waters, and at least 20 have none.

AI's water bill depends on who counts

2030 estimates for data centres run from 644 billion litres (direct cooling) to 9.3 trillion litres (the water footprint of their electricity), a spread driven by definitions.

Why it matters

Without a common metric, governments cannot price or regulate data-centre water use. A disclosure standard is a concrete, low-cost outcome the conference could back.

The money is the bottleneck

Meeting SDG 6 needs more than US$1 trillion a year. Over 90% of water finance comes from public budgets, and private capital is under 2% of water investment in low- and middle-income countries.
