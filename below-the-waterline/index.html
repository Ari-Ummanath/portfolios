Live research brief · 2026 UN Water Conference · Abu Dhabi, 8–10 December

Every water decision is a security decision.

The world's third UN water conference in about fifty years meets in a region where desalination plants have been hit in an active war, violence over water is at a record high, and AI is competing with cities for cooling water. This brief reads the conference through three lenses: water as security, water and conflict, and Australia and the Indo-Pacific.

By Ari · International Security Studies & Arts (Criminology), Australian National University ·
