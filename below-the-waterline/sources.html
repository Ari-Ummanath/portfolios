Method

Primary documents come first: UN resolutions and concept papers, government advisories, and peer-reviewed or institutional reports. News reports are used for dated events and are attributed. Where sources disagree, both are shown and the difference explained.

Lines marked “Analysis” and the “possible solution” for each dialogue are the author's judgement, built from the options in the official papers and the evidence in this brief.
