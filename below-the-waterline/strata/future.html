The 25 countries already at the edge

These countries regularly withdraw at least 80% of their renewable supply, WRI's threshold for extremely high water stress. Together they hold a quarter of the world's population. In the Middle East and North Africa, 83% of people already live with extremely high water stress; in South Asia, 74%.

    What bends the curve

    The record also holds recoveries

    Cape Town more than halved its daily water use in three years and never reached Day Zero. A dam completed in 2005 let the northern Aral Sea partly recover. Projections are not destiny; they assume today's management continues.

    Money matters too: the World Bank puts the water infrastructure gap at US$6.7 trillion by 2030. Where that money is pledged is the subject of the 2026 UN Water Conference, tracked live in Below the Waterline.

    Every collapse on the Civilisations page happened without early warning, finance or treaties. Today's societies have all three. The open question is whether they are used at the scale the projections describe.
