Simulator · 3000 BCE to 2100

Five thousand years of thirst, on one map.

Each marker is a documented case where water insecurity shaped a society, from the Akkadian Empire to Cape Town's “Day Zero”, and on to published projections for 2050 and beyond.

How to read the map

Markers appear when the evidence says they should

A filled, pulsing marker is a case active in the selected year. Once a case ends it leaves a faint hollow ring, so the map accumulates history as you move forward. From 2023 onward, sand-coloured countries are the 25 that WRI's Aqueduct atlas rates as facing extremely high water stress every year. Dashed markers are published projections, not forecasts made here.

Read the full cases on the Civilisations and Modern era pages.

Water insecurity rarely acts alone. In every case here, scholars debate how much drought mattered compared with politics, war, trade and inequality. The evidence shows when water failed; it rarely settles why a society broke.
