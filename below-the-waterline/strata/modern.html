Modern era · 1960 to 2026

When people became the driver

In the instrumental era we can measure drought directly, and human choices move to the centre: rivers diverted for irrigation, reservoirs managed for some users and not others, and water infrastructure destroyed in war.

The modern signal

Violence over water is at a record

The Pacific Institute's database tracks cases where water is a trigger, a weapon or a casualty of violence. It records 420 events in 2024, against 24 in 2000, and now holds more than 2,750 incidents.

Part of the rise may reflect better reporting, since the database is built from news and open sources. Even so, the direction matches the cases below: water is increasingly a target, not only scarce.
