About

About me

I'm completing a double degree in International Security Studies and Arts (Criminology) at the ANU, graduating in 2027. My work sits where policy, technology and security meet: export controls, defence assurance, economic security, and the integrity of institutions people rely on.

I've written a cyber strategy brief for the Czech Ambassador during an internship with the Czech Embassy and EU Delegation, written policy briefs for a national policy library at FORE Australia, and served as Policy & Strategic Affairs Fellow with Girls Run The World. I speak English, Malayalam and Hindi.

Every project here was self-directed: I start from a documented, verifiable gap, read the primary sources, and build something that reflects that reading.
