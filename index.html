About me

I'm completing a double degree in International Security Studies and Arts (Criminology) at the Australian National University, graduating in 2027. My work sits where policy, technology and security meet: export controls, defence assurance, economic security, and the integrity of institutions people rely on.

Every project here was self-directed: I start from a documented, verifiable gap, read the primary sources, and build something that reflects that reading.
