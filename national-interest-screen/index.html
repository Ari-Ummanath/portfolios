The National Interest Screen

Australia screens foreign investment case by case against the national interest. Since the 2024 reforms, the system runs on two tracks: faster decisions for low-risk investors, and closer scrutiny where a deal touches critical minerals, critical infrastructure, critical technology, sensitive data or land near government facilities. This worksheet walks a hypothetical deal through that logic, from whether it must be notified to which national interest factors carry the weight.
