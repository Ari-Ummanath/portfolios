The Phoenix Signal

Illegal phoenix activity is when a company is deliberately wound up to dodge tax, wage and super debts, and its business is carried on by a new entity. This tool turns publicly reported warning signs into a simple triage read-out, then maps the result to the ATO's graduated response: make compliance easy, tailor help, and make sure deliberate non-compliance has consequences.

Graduated response pathway

  1. Make it easyEducation, prompts and clear guidance for businesses in genuine difficulty.
  2. Tailored help and early engagementContact, payment arrangements, director-obligation reminders.
  3. Compliance review or auditData-matching across agencies; review of asset transfers and related entities.
  4. ConsequencesDirector penalties and disqualification; referral of the most serious cases to the Serious Financial Crime Taskforce.

Method. Indicator weights are my own illustrative scoring for teaching purposes and are not ATO methodology. A single indicator rarely proves anything; legitimate restructures and honest business failures share several of these signs. The aim is to show how layered signals, not any one fact, separate misfortune from a deliberate pattern, which mirrors the ATO view that illegal phoenixing becomes the concern when it is repeated and cyclical.
