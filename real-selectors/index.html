Why these two, together

The same question, read twice

Cricket Canada shows what happens with no separation between who controls a federation and who it lets play: a board itself allegedly captured, deciding selection. Cricket Australia is trying to build that separation deliberately — sell the BBL's equity, keep the calendar and the salary cap — over the objection of the players' own association. Neither case is finished. Both are about the same asset: a contracted cricketer, and who actually gets to decide what happens to them.
