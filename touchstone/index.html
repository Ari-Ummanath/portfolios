Every model needs a hallmark.

For seven centuries, silver has been tested by an independent assay office before it could be sold as sterling. AI has no equivalent. Nobody outside a lab can verify what silicon a model was trained on, what data went in, or whether something was planted along the way. Touchstone is that office: an independent nonprofit that tests the AI supply chain end to end and stamps what passes.

01 · The problem

AI is built on a chain nobody can see the whole of.

A frontier model is the end product of a supply chain longer than most people realise: chips fabricated in one country, packaged in another, flashed with firmware, racked in a datacentre, fed trillions of tokens scraped from a web anyone can write to, refined with synthetic data generated by other models, then shipped as weights through public hubs into agents that read live documents. Each link can be compromised. Most are verified only by the party with the most to lose from finding a problem.

The data link is the most alarming. The 2025 poisoning study overturned the old assumption that an attacker must control a fixed share of the training set; what matters is an absolute count, so the attacker's job stays flat while the dataset grows. The hardware link is the most politically live: US prosecutors charged three people tied to Super Micro in March 2026 over an alleged $2.5B scheme to move Nvidia-equipped servers to China, and the Chip Security Act would mandate location verification on exported AI chips.

04 · The organisation

Four programmes, one office.

Touchstone is a nonprofit research foundation with a public-benefit assay arm. The foundation owns the standard and the research, so no customer can buy a softer test. The assay arm charges for audits, and its surplus flows back to the foundation. The precedent is Underwriters Laboratories, set up in 1894 with insurance-industry backing to test electrical products, which turned a public-safety problem into a self-funding institution.

Tr

Trace · the open standard

A signed AI Bill of Materials linking silicon attestation, crawl manifests, synthetic-data lineage and weight hashes into one verifiable record.

  • Hash-pinned dataset snapshots and signed crawl manifests
  • Lineage tags for synthetic data: which model made it, from what prompt set
  • Built to slot into existing work at CoSAI, OWASP and NIST, not compete with it
Ca

Canary · the friendly attack

The intriguing part. Touchstone runs the attack harmlessly, so labs learn whether they would have caught it.

  • Benign, registered canary documents seeded on the open web under a published protocol
  • Labs opt in to learn whether canaries reached their training sets
  • Open backdoor-scanning bench: detection tools tested against known-planted models
As

Assay · the Mark

Independent audits against Trace, with four separate punches so buyers see exactly what was verified.

  • Si hardware provenance and attestation · D data provenance and poison screening
  • W weight integrity and security level · Op deployment, retrieval and agent hygiene
  • Date letter on every mark: assays expire and must be renewed
Se

Sentinel · the exchange

An information-sharing and analysis centre for the AI supply chain, modelled on the financial sector's FS-ISAC.

  • Members report poisoned sources, compromised packages and tampered hardware confidentially
  • Touchstone anonymises and redistributes within hours
  • Feeds Australia's AI Safety Institute and partner institutes through agreed channels

06 · The ambitious part

A flywheel that makes security the cheaper choice.

  1. Research exposes how poisoning and hardware tampering actually work at scale.
  2. Findings harden into Trace, a standard labs can implement cheaply.
  3. Labs and enterprises buy assays to prove they meet it.
  4. The cunning lever: insurers price AI liability using the Mark, so an unmarked model costs more to cover. Security stops being a cost centre.
  5. Governments cite the Mark in procurement, as they already do with hardware certifications.
  6. Every assay and member report feeds Sentinel, which sharpens the research. The loop tightens.
07 · Why it matters beyond business

The catastrophic-risk case.

Funders such as Coefficient Giving's Project Tailwind ask a hard question: how does the work actually reduce catastrophic risk, and who will change their decisions because of it? Touchstone's answer has three parts. A backdoored model wired into power grids, defence systems or biology tooling is a catastrophe waiting for a trigger phrase. Poisoned alignment and safety-training data could quietly undo the safeguards labs believe they have. And stolen or diverted compute and weights proliferate capability to actors who will not test anything.

Each programme has a named user who changes a decision. Lab security teams change what data they ingest after a Canary result. Procurement officers change what they buy when the Mark is in the tender. Underwriters change premiums. Safety institutes change what they test for when Sentinel shows them what's circulating. Research that nobody acts on is excluded by design: every Touchstone output ships with the decision it is meant to change.

AU

Why Canberra

Australia is a trusted middle power inside Five Eyes and AUKUS, with no frontier lab of its own to protect. That makes an Australian assay office credible to US, UK and Asian labs alike. Australia's AI Safety Institute became operational in early 2026 with $29.9M, began testing frontier models in July and signed a cooperation MoU with the UK institute in May. Touchstone gives that ecosystem an independent, non-government partner with supply-chain depth.

09 · What could go wrong

The risks, faced squarely.

Capture

Paying customers lean on auditors. Mitigation: the foundation owns the standard, assay staff rotate, and failed assays are counted publicly even when details stay private.

Dual use

Poisoning research teaches attackers too. Mitigation: canaries are benign and registered, methods go through coordinated disclosure, and the most sensitive findings go to labs and institutes first.

Legal exposure

Seeding content on the open web raises terms-of-service and liability questions. Mitigation: legal review is budgeted from day one, and canaries are placed only on domains Touchstone controls or partners consent to.

Lab refusal

Frontier labs may decline outside audits. Mitigation: start with open-weight labs and enterprise buyers, then let insurers and procurement create the pull.

10 · The ask
Draft expression of interest · Project Tailwind

Touchstone is an independent assay office for the AI supply chain. Recent research shows a near-constant handful of poisoned documents can backdoor language models regardless of size, and advanced chips are being diverted despite export controls, yet no independent body verifies what hardware, data and weights a model is built from. Touchstone will publish an open provenance standard (Trace), run benign canary experiments that tell labs whether they would have caught a poisoning attack, audit models against the standard, and operate a threat-sharing exchange for the AI supply chain. Based in Canberra and working with Australia's AI Safety Institute and allied institutes, we seek $1.5M in pre-seed funding over 18 months to build the founding team, ship the threat map and complete a first canary pilot with two open-weight labs.
